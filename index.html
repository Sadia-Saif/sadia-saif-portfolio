From a cold contact
to a closed client

I own the whole sales pipeline myself, so nothing gets lost between the first list and the signed deal.

  1. 1

    Find the right accounts

    I build lists in LinkedIn Sales Navigator and Apollo.io, and keep only companies with a real operational or automation need.

  2. 2

    Write the first message

    Connection notes and cold emails written for one person at a time, leading with value instead of a pitch.

  3. 3

    Follow up

    Structured sequences on LinkedIn and email, with objections answered plainly as they come up.

  4. 4

    Run the discovery call

    I turn replies into discovery calls, and use them to understand what the client needs and map it to the right solution.

  5. 5

    Send the proposal

    A clear proposal and cost estimate, followed by meetings where I answer questions and handle objections.

  6. 6

    Negotiate and close

    I negotiate terms and close the client, then brief the delivery team so the project starts with the requirements intact.

Then I measure it. Acceptance, reply and meeting-booked rates go into the CRM, and the next round of targeting and messaging changes based on what they show.

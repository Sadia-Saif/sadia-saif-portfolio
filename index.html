From a cold contact to a booked call

I own the whole outreach funnel myself, so nothing gets lost between the list and the meeting.

  1. Find the right accounts

    I build lists in LinkedIn Sales Navigator and Apollo.io, and keep only companies with a real operational or automation need.

  2. Write the first message

    Connection notes and cold emails written for one person at a time, leading with value instead of a pitch.

  3. Follow up

    Structured sequences on LinkedIn and email, with objections answered plainly as they come up.

  4. Book the call and hand over

    I turn replies into discovery calls, then brief the delivery team so the client's requirements arrive intact.
